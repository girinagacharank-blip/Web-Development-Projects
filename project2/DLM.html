Welcome to your digital library

Open Shelf is a digital library interface for exploring books and learning about borrowing. This project demonstrates how HTML can organize a library catalogue, featured titles, categories, and library information.

Membership and Library Rules

The membership details and rules below are sample content.

Sample membership information

  • Sample membership is open to students and staff of Open Shelf.
  • A sample member may borrow up to four books at a time.
  • A sample library card is used to associate loans with a member.

Sample borrowing and return rules

  • The sample borrowing period is 14 calendar days.
  • Members should return or renew borrowed books by the due date.
  • Reference books in this sample are for in-library use only.
  • Sample fine: ₹5 per book for each day it is returned late.
  • Sample replacement charge for a lost or damaged book: ₹500.
